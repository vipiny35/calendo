Brand marks for the meeting services a calendar link can point at, used to
identify the service on the popover's join row. Each is the owner's own logo,
downscaled to 44px tall from Wikimedia Commons; the trademarks belong to
Google, Zoom, Microsoft, Cisco and Apple respectively.

Zoom and Webex publish wordmarks rather than square icons, so those two are
wider than they are tall and the join row sizes marks by height.
